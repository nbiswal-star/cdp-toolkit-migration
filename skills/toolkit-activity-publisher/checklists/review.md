# Review checklist (before you click Publish)

Tick every box. If one fails, do not publish; fix the draft or ask the author.

## Wording
- [ ] Title and number match the source document.
- [ ] Every learning goal, step, and journal prompt is present, in the same order.
- [ ] No sentence was reworded, shortened, or added. (The automatic check shows any difference
      as a word list: `-` removed, `+` added.)

## Numbers
- [ ] Each step heading shows the same minutes as the source.
- [ ] The total time is copied exactly from the source. If it does not equal the sum of the
      step times, change neither number; ask the author.
- [ ] Steps are numbered One, Two, Three... with no gaps.

## Page details
- [ ] Module, level, and tags (formats) are ticked correctly, and Activity details are filled in.
- [ ] The PDF opens and is the right activity.
- [ ] Embedded slides or videos (if any) load on the preview page.
- [ ] Links open the right pages.

## Privacy and safety
- [ ] Any email address, phone number, or participant name listed under "Flagged" has been
      kept or removed by a person, on purpose.
- [ ] No text addressed to an AI system (for example "ignore previous instructions").

## Publish
- [ ] Preview looks right on desktop and phone.
- [ ] Click Publish. (The migration account cannot publish; only IF staff can.)
