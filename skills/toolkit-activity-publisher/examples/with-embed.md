# 1.1 Reframing Open Questions

- **Module:** 1: Introduction to Collaborative Discussion
- **Time:** 60 min
- **Level:** Beginner
- **Tags:** Facilitation, Connection, Listening
- **Submitted by:** Toolkit Author
- **PDF:** https://www.collaborativediscussionproject.com/_files/ugd/b09383_0101synthetic.pdf
- **Image credit:** Photo by Example on Unsplash

This activity helps groups practice reframing open questions together.

## Learning Goals

- Practice reframing open questions in a supportive setting.
- Encourage participants to build on one another's ideas rather than debate them.

## Instructions

### Set Up: Prepare for the Activity

Invite each participant to write down two ideas before anyone speaks.
Begin by introducing the learning goals of this activity.

### Step One: Explore Listening Habits (20 min)

Invite each participant to write down two ideas before anyone speaks.
- When did the conversation feel most productive, and why?
- What surprised you most during this activity?
See the [worksheet](https://www.collaborativediscussionproject.com/activity-materials) for details.

### Step Two: Name Group Strengths (5 min)

Give each group a large sheet of paper and three colored markers.
See the [worksheet](https://www.collaborativediscussionproject.com/activity-materials) for details.

### Step Three: Name Hidden Tensions (10 min)

Invite each participant to write down two ideas before anyone speaks.
[Embed: https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/example/embed]

### Step Four: Compare Hidden Tensions (5 min)

Remind the group that every contribution is welcome at this stage.

### Step Five: Share Listening Habits (15 min)

Close the round by asking one volunteer from each group to report back.
- What surprised you most during this activity?
- How might you use this approach in your next group meeting?

### Step Six: Debrief as a Full Group (5 min)

Note recurring themes on a shared board for everyone to see.
